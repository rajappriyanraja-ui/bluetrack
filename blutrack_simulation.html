PHASE-BY-PHASE STRUCTURAL BREAKDOWN
Inorganic Substrate Engineering Guide
LAYER 06
Hard Coat (Durabis)
A 50μm shielding layer designed to isolate the sensitive optical surface from mechanical abrasion, chemicals, and finger oils.
Impact: 50x Scratch Resistance
LAYER 05
Cover Layer
Precision optically clear polycarbonate. Its exact 100μm profile is the key to minimizing spherical aberration for the high-NA blue laser.
Tolerance: ±2μm uniformity
LAYER 04
Recording Layer
Inorganic Phase-Change material (Cu/Si). Heat from the 405nm laser flips material states to encode binary 1s (crystalline) and 0s (amorphous).
Material: Inorganic Alloy
LAYER 03
Reflective Layer
High-reflectivity silver alloy (or gold in archival media). Serves as the return path for the laser pickup to interpret the pit sequence.
Function: Laser beam return
LAYER 02
Data Layer (L0)
The physical molding of pits and lands. Blu-ray's 0.32μm track pitch is the breakthrough that enables 25GB per standard layer.
Density: 25GB Capacity
LAYER 01
Polycarbonate Substrate
The 1.1mm foundation providing structural rigidity. Since the laser does not pass through this base, the disc is immune to substrate-induced coma.
Base: 1.1mm Rigid Plastic
SYSTEM INSIGHTS & ACTIONS
BluTrack Reference Data
  • BER Standards: 100% = <1×10⁻¹¹ through 50% = >1×10⁻⁴ errors/bit.
  • Scratch Damage: From 0% (factory) to 38%+ (severe) surface defects.
  • Oxidation Index: Iridescence loss from 0% (pristine) to 60%+ (disc rot).
  • Jitter Thresholds: Standards allow <9dB; >12dB indicates failure.
Forensic Advisory
  • Hard Coating: Blu-ray's TDK Durabis layer is 50x more scratch-resistant than DVD.
  • Oxidation Risk: Silver-alloy reflective layers in cheap blanks rot faster than Gold/Silicon.
  • Track Pitch: 0.32μm pitch requires precise laser alignment; physical warp is fatal.
Longevity Protocol
  • Vertical Storage: Prevents "disc creep" or substrate warping over decades.
  • Thermal Control: Every 5°C drop in ambient temp doubles the Arrhenius lifespan.
  • Verification: Perform a binary diff every 5 years to ensure zero bit-rot.
1 Blu-ray disc captures the equivalent data of 36 legacy CDs — significantly reducing physical waste.
